Fundamental Rules

Three rules, no exceptions

These rules govern every product, every workflow, every release.

  1. 01

    Customers buy from One Stop Uniforms.

    Supplier identities remain confidential. The brand customers trust is the only brand they see.

  2. 02

    Every public product receives an internal SKU, product ID, URL, pricing, metadata, and search index.

    Vendor identifiers remain internal. Our catalog is the canonical source of truth.

    The customer database is one of the company's most valuable assets.

    Every feature should strengthen customer retention. Long-term relationships outrank short-term wins.
