Project Philosophy

Enterprise software, not an ecommerce site

The platform shall be built as enterprise software rather than a basic ecommerce website. Long-term architecture takes precedence over short-term convenience.

“The right architecture today is what makes the next ten years possible.”

Fundamental Rules

Three rules, no exceptions

These rules govern every product, every workflow, every release.

  1. 01

    Customers buy from One Stop Uniforms.

    Supplier identities remain confidential. The brand customers trust is the only brand they see.

  2. 02

    Every public product receives an internal SKU, product ID, URL, pricing, metadata, and search index.

    Vendor identifiers remain internal. Our catalog is the canonical source of truth.

  3. 03

    The customer database is one of the company's most valuable assets.

    Every feature should strengthen customer retention. Long-term relationships outrank short-term wins.

Purpose

Every customer-facing capability, defined

Define every customer-facing capability of the One Stop Uniforms Enterprise Platform — so that no surface, no persona, and no workflow is left to interpretation by future volumes.

Customer Personas

Who the platform is built for

Procurement managers, HR teams, school administrators, hospital buyers, hotel managers, restaurant owners, finance approvers, employees, and administrators. Every workflow is modeled against these real-world roles.

  • Procurement Managers

    Centralized purchasing, contract enforcement, supplier visibility.

  • HR Teams

    Employee wardrobe programs and onboarding uniforms, automated.

  • School Administrators

    Student & faculty uniforms, parent pay, store credit.

  • Hospital Buyers

    Department-based ordering, scrub programs, infection-control compliance.

  • Hotel Managers

    Departmental uniform programs with seasonal reorders.

  • Restaurant Owners

    Front-of-house and back-of-house programs with embroidery workflows.

  • Finance Approvers

    Budget controls, multi-stage approvals, audit-grade history.

  • Employees

    Self-service ordering within approved catalogs and allowances.

  • Administrators

    Catalog, account, store, and policy administration.

Organization Accounts

Hierarchies, built in

Support parent companies, multiple locations, departments, buyers, approvers, finance contacts, and custom catalogs. Org structure is a first-class model — not a bolt-on.

Shopping Experience

Procurement, not retail

Tools optimized for how organizations actually order — not how consumers do.
